# Changelog

Versions of the landing page. Each one is tagged `vX.Y.Z` in git, and the
version is also in the `<meta name="version">` tag of every page.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-21

### Changed
- Privacy policy: the contact address for questions,
  bluff.game.support@gmail.com.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-11-21

### Changed
- Bluff comes to Steam for Windows first. Linux follows once it has been
  tested on a real Linux desktop.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-01

### Changed
- Privacy policy: ghost votes are gone from the game, so they are no longer
  listed in what other players receive.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-31

### Changed
- Privacy policy: online play goes through Steam rooms only, so other
  players never see your IP address. Removed the direct-connect IP line.
- Privacy policy: your player name is your Steam name unless you set
  another in Settings.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-28

### Added
- Landing page and privacy policy, with self-hosted fonts.
