A game of poker hands and nerve

Bluff

Every claim is a lie — until the cards say otherwise.

Coming to Steam · Windows

The game

Sit down at a table in the Mask Room with up to five other players. Everyone holds a few hidden cards. On your turn, claim a poker hand that's somewhere among all the cards at the table, or call bluff on the last claim. Read faces, voices and nerves: the loser of every call takes another card, and the sixth card puts you out.

Features

  • Up to 6 players online with friends through Steam, or solo against bots.
  • Proximity voice chat from each player's seat, with lip sync.
  • Characters whose heads and eyes follow where each player really looks.
  • Knocked out? Stay at the table as a ghost: your card still counts and your voice still carries.
