Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Bluff is made by Stoyan Grozdanov ("we"). We don't collect, store or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

On your computer

Your settings (volume, graphics, controls, player name) and the game's logs are saved on your own computer. They are never sent to us. You may send us logs yourself, for example with a bug report.

Playing online

Online matches are hosted by a player. No server of ours is in between. The other players' games receive:

  • Your player name. In Steam rooms this is your Steam name.
  • What you do in the game: claims, bluff calls, where you look, taunts and ghost votes.
  • Your voice, only while you use voice chat. It is sent live and played immediately, and is not recorded or stored by the game, by us or by other players' games.
  • Your IP address, in direct-connect rooms. Steam rooms connect through Steam, which hides your IP address from other players.

We cannot see, moderate or record online matches. You can mute any player from the pause menu, turn voice chat off in Settings > Voice, and play only with friends in Steam rooms, which only your Steam friends and the people you invite can see.

Steam

Valve handles your Steam account, friends list, invites, lobbies and voice relay under the Steam Privacy Policy. The game reads your Steam name and Steam ID to show your name, put you in rooms and show friends what you're doing (for example "At the table, 4 players left").

Children

Bluff is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Steam's age requirements and parental controls apply, and the game respects Steam's voice chat restrictions.
